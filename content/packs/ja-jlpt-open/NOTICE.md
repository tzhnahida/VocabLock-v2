# OpenJLPT N5–N1 sample pack

7,811 Japanese words tagged with JLPT levels, readings, parts of speech and
example sentences. Generated from OpenJLPT.

- OpenJLPT: https://github.com/evanclan/openjlpt — data licensed
  CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- JLPT levels follow Jonathan Waller's community lists
  (https://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/), CC BY.
- Example sentences come from Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), CC BY 2.0 FR.

The JLPT has not published official word lists since 2010; treat the levels
as a well-informed approximation. This pack is content, not framework: drop
it into `content/packs/` to enable it, delete the folder to remove it.

Rebuild with `content/tools/build-jlpt-pack.py`.
