This content pack bundles a 300-entry sample extracted from JMdict.

- Source: JMdict (https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html), release 3.6.2,
  via https://github.com/scriptin/jmdict-simplified (common subset).
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0).
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Changes: a subset of entries was selected and reduced to word, reading,
  part of speech, and up to three glosses per sense.

This pack is a demo of the content-pack format, not a complete dictionary.
